## Behavior and architecture

`SkyUfoThrusters` uses four authored local slots at `(±5,-2,±5)` in the existing 1046-cell hull. These are ordinary `SHIP_THRUSTER` blocks in its local grid, with no additional RigidBody or BlockEntity. Each applies force at its transformed cell centre. `D6ThrusterMixer` accounts for the current centre of mass when distributing the requested force and torque; the actual world torque arises from those mounted impulses.

Each pod has a fixed authored capacity of three times the template's solid-cell count. Added mass cannot manufacture engine power. A shared saturation factor bounds the full nominal bank. A missing engine contributes nothing; surviving engines do not gain its force. Partial damage therefore permits a physical imbalance. Cutting every engine leaves inertial travel and rotation intact, while held pilot inputs and AI cannot accelerate the craft through a bypass. The actual reactor must also be present for drive authority to apply thrust.

A new craft uses physical-UFO schema2. Ordinary prismarine inserted in an engine slot cannot power it. Repair uses collision-checked local block placement; mass and geometry update through existing BlockBody access. The engine has a block/item model, ru/en name, recipe, loot and mining tags. Its current model uses vanilla's lit blast-furnace face texture; native exterior frames show the world block and held/hotbar item without missing textures. Engine VFX and custom artwork are separate work.

Schema1 physical craft retain the function of surviving original prismarine engine-slot cells. This compatibility path does not insert material, repair a missing slot, increment the grid revision or change pose/momentum on read. Subsequent saving records schema2 and the compatibility flag. This does not certify old prephysical/schema0 or contextless world migration.
