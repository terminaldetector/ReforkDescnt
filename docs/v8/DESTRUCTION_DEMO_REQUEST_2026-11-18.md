Demonstrate rocket breaches, a bomb opening a load-bearing section, subsequent collapse, severed/falling trees, fragment contact and settling, with useful close and wide camera views. Reuse the ordinary server simulation and game assets.

Deliver actual gameplay footage and selected frames from the same identified build, with a short description of weapons/settings and observed limits. Record projectile impact, material removal, fragment separation, inherited motion, impacts and remaining structure. Preserve item/debris budgets and note observed frame/server performance rather than promising equivalence to Teardown.

No demonstration has been recorded yet. Complete any missing physical response exposed by the scene before presenting it as working. This request does not accept full city-scale destruction, all tree morphology, every material or the complete specifications.
