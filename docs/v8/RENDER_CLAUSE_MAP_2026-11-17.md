Scope clarification: the specification permits approximate collision proxies; absence of triangle collision alone is not a failed requirement and does not authorize a new physics engine. Vanilla single-height cloud rendering also exists; the open atmospheric item is altitude/farLOD-aware cloud/fade behaviour, not a claim that the game has no clouds. Conservative caps and existing capsule/simple-tree paths are valid partial implementation and must be preserved.
