# Afterburner — 4 accelerator tiers (traffic light)

Hold **R** for Descent cruise afterburner. Tier picks the burn grade; engine energy allocation still scales inside each tier.

| Tier | Color | Cost /s | Role |
|-----:|-------|--------:|------|
| 1 | Green | 4 | Eco cruise |
| 2 | Yellow | 6 | Stock Descent (default) |
| 3 | Orange | 9 | Hot |
| 4 | Red | 14 | Max burn |

## Customize menus

| Mode | Open | Contents |
|------|------|----------|
| Survival (light) | **N** or Options → Ship / Afterburner | Tier 1–4 + energy presets |
| Creative (full) | same | + accel / drag / max-speed sliders |

Commands: `/d6 afterburner` · `/d6 afterburner <1-4>`

HUD shows `AFTERBURNER ON Y2` (etc.) in the tier color while burning.
